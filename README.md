# 🧵 Herfa — UI/UX Case Study

## 📎 Project Name

**Herfa (حِرفة)** — Egyptian Handicrafts E-commerce Platform

## 📌 Project Overview

Herfa is an e-commerce platform dedicated to promoting and selling Egyptian handmade crafts, connecting local artisans with customers who appreciate authentic, traditional, and culturally inspired products. The platform focuses on empowering Egyptian artisans by providing them with a digital marketplace to showcase their work, share their stories, and reach a wider audience.

The platform highlights the story behind each product and its creator through storytelling, helping preserve Egyptian cultural heritage and increase the perceived value of handmade products. It also allows customers to request custom designs tailored to their preferences.

This UI/UX case study documents the design process, including user research, market and competitor analysis, identifying user needs and pain points, developing user personas, organizing the user journey, and designing an intuitive and culturally inspired user interface.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Understand the needs, behaviors, and challenges of Egyptian artisans and customers.
* Identify the difficulties artisans face in digital marketing and selling handmade products online.
* Conduct market research and competitor analysis to identify opportunities in the handmade crafts market.
* Create user personas and understand the expectations of both artisans and customers.
* Design an intuitive and user-friendly e-commerce experience for discovering and purchasing handmade crafts.
* Highlight the stories of artisans and their products to communicate cultural value and authenticity.
* Enable customers to request customized handmade products through custom orders.
* Support local artisans, preserve Egyptian cultural heritage, and promote economic empowerment through digital commerce.

## 📦 Project Scope
